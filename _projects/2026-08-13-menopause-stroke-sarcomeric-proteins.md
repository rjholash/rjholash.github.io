This 2026 undergraduate summer research project investigated the effects of menopause and stroke on skeletal-muscle sarcomeric proteins in a rat model. The work was led by **Faraja Herve Habiyakare** with support from a Program for Undergraduate Research Experience (PURE) Award.

## Research Focus

Sarcomeric proteins form the molecular machinery that allows skeletal muscle to generate force. Changes to their organization or composition may help explain how muscle function is affected by physiological transitions and neurological injury.

Faraja's project examined this molecular component of muscle health in the combined context of menopause and stroke. It complemented parallel work on muscle weakness by helping connect functional outcomes with the proteins responsible for muscle contraction.
