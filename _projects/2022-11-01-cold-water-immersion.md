This project explored the physiological consequences of cold water immersion as a recovery strategy for endurance athletes. Students collected data on lactate clearance, heart rate variability, and perceived recovery scores across multiple immersion protocols.

## Research Background

Cold water immersion has gained popularity as a recovery modality among athletes, but questions remain about optimal protocols. Our research focuses on quantifying the physiological responses to different water temperatures and immersion durations.

## Methodology

We use a combination of physiological markers to assess recovery:
- Blood lactate clearance rates
- Heart rate variability metrics
- Core and skin temperature changes
- Subjective recovery scores

## Key Findings

Our preliminary results suggest that brief (5-10 minute) immersions at moderate temperatures (10-15°C) may offer optimal benefits for endurance athletes without the discomfort associated with more extreme protocols.
