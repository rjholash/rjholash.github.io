The ACTIVATION research program and ACTIVATION Health platform contributed to the successful acquisition of a **$75,000 Alberta Spine Foundation research grant** led by **Dr. Fred Nicholls**.

This award extends the ACTIVATION program into spine-surgery and rehabilitation research, building on the platform's ability to collect longitudinal activity, recovery, and patient-reported outcome data through secure digital-health infrastructure.
