Following McCaig Institute SPARK Grant support, the ACTIVATION research program began development of a secure mobile-health platform for longitudinal activity tracking, questionnaire delivery, wearable-device integration, and research-data management.

The platform development work established the technical foundation for **ACTIVATION Health**, supporting studies in clinical recovery, oncology, rehabilitation, and patient-reported outcomes while creating opportunities for student research and software-development training.
