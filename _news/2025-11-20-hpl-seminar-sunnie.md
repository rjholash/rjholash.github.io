NSERC summer student **Sunnie Vuong** presented her research on "Structural Characteristics of Adductor Longus and Gracilis from Children with Cerebral Palsy" at the **Human Performance Lab Seminar** on November 20, 2025, alongside summer student Priyal from Dr. Walter Herzog's lab. This was Sunnie's fourth presentation of her summer research, following earlier appearances at the McCaig Institute Symposium, Transdisciplinary Child & Maternal Health Research Day, and the 26th Annual AB BME Conference.
