Honours student **Andrew Filewich** completed high-quality demonstration videos and interactive learning modules for KNES 375: Tests and Measurements in Kinesiology as part of the MELT grant research program. His project involved filming KNES 375 activities and developing multimedia supports for blood pressure and heart-rate assessment, mCAFT testing, and Jackson-Pollock skinfold procedures. His poster is available here: [AFilewich_Thesis_Poster_Final.pptx](/docs/AFilewich_Thesis_Poster_Final.pptx).
