**ACTIVATION Health** was featured by UCalgary News on September 17, 2026, in an article about how the made-at-UCalgary platform aims to change the measurement of recovery.

The research program addresses a practical limitation in clinical assessment: recovery does not happen only during appointments. Mobility, symptoms, physical activity, and patient-reported outcomes change throughout daily life, while conventional follow-up captures only occasional snapshots.

ACTIVATION Health was developed to help research teams study that broader recovery experience. Its University of Calgary-hosted infrastructure supports participant enrollment and consent, questionnaires, longitudinal mobility and activity tracking, wearable-device integration, and research-data management.

The platform grew from the ACTIVATION research program—**Activity Capture To Investigate Voluntary Activity in Oncology and Normal Populations**—and now supports work involving musculoskeletal oncology, sarcoma, spine surgery, orthopaedics, and rehabilitation.

The UCalgary feature marks an important public milestone for a project that has developed from seed-funded research into a working digital-health platform and Apple App Store release.
